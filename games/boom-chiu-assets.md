# Bùm Chíu — Free asset sources

Only free / attribution-optional assets are approved for this game.

## UI / HUD

- **Kenney UI Pack** — Creative Commons CC0, 430+ UI sprites. Free for personal and commercial use, attribution not required.
  - https://kenney.nl/assets/ui-pack
- **Kenney Crosshair Pack** — Creative Commons CC0, 200+ crosshairs plus vector source. Free for personal and commercial use, attribution not required.
  - https://kenney.nl/assets/crosshair-pack
- **Kenney Shooting Gallery** — Creative Commons CC0, HUD/background/shooter UI pieces.
  - https://kenney.nl/assets/shooting-gallery

## 3D / future renderer upgrade

- **Quaternius Universal Base Characters** — free for personal, educational and commercial projects; CC0 pack page.
  - https://quaternius.com/packs/universalbasecharacters.html
- **Quaternius Universal Animation Library** — 120+ animations including gun/combat locomotion; free/CC0 pack page.
  - https://quaternius.com/packs/universalanimationlibrary.html
- **Quaternius Sci‑Fi Essentials Kit** — free/CC0 pack page with guns, crates and environment props.
  - https://quaternius.com/packs/scifiessentialskit.html

## Policy

Do not import Counter-Strike maps, weapon models, logos, sounds, textures or character art. Layouts and visuals must remain original. Third-party assets may only be committed after their license is verified as CC0 or otherwise free for commercial use without a paid dependency.
